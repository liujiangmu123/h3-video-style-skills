# Domain Kits — Ready-to-Use Anchor Kits per Field

> Migrated 2026-09-05 from `shenhai-style-explainer/references/` into this planning skill: kits are *planning data* (which fields fit, what their alphabet is), not style rules. The generation skill receives the relevant kit through the 任务消息 / 开工种子包 and, when used standalone, re-derives one with the five-step method kept in `shenhai-style-explainer/references/style-dna.md` B-0. Section order matches `domain-ledger.md` numbering loosely; use the ledger for status, this file for content. The Four Gates referenced below live in `style-dna.md` B-0 (downward reference only).

Core formula: **Shenhai style = fixed animation grammar × domain visual alphabet × the register (how much world the frame holds).** The grammar (zero-cut morph, one-sentence-one-event, elastic easing, monochrome + accent, an empty lower fifth for post-production subtitles, concept cards, calm narration) never changes. Swapping domains means swapping the visual alphabet: the cold-open atom, the demo materials, and the anchor containers all come from the field's own graphic vocabulary — and swapping the register when the field's knowledge lives somewhere other than signs.

Shenhai's own subject is graphic design, so dots/lines/planes ARE his domain content. Every other field must supply its own alphabet. Rule of thumb: **if the knowledge could be explained on a blackboard with drawings, it fits; if it must be filmed to be believed, it does not.**

Fit criteria: visual alphabet exists / morph potential / case ladder (history → classics → modern product) / anchor recognizability / concept-term density. **v2 additions for the five-star bar:** topic-pool depth (≥50 non-repeating episodes — sustains a long-running series) and mass appeal (a hook for viewers outside the field: exam demand, daily curiosity, or hot topics). Style-fit alone without pool depth or mass appeal caps at four stars — fine for one-off episodes, not a main series.

**v3 (2026-09-05) — the alphabet test is necessary but not sufficient.** Before accepting a domain (and before accepting each *episode* inside a domain), run the Four Gates in `style-dna.md` B-0: (1) a drawable atom, (2) a principle demonstrable in 20s without words, (3) a real object the principle's skeleton can be drawn over, (4) an instance the viewer owns. The kits below list the alphabet; the gates decide whether the philosophy (show-before-name, evidence with skeleton, principle back in the viewer's hand) can actually be executed. Episode lists for a domain should be built by enumerating principles that pass gate 2 and pairing each with a gate-3 object and a gate-4 object.

**v4 (2026-10-01) — a container is not a demonstration; the register is part of the kit.** A film-language episode built from this file's old kit (a frame, timeline tracks, a clapperboard) and drawn the originals' way — dots for people, one wire, two clip-art birds — passed alignment and read as "元素极其简单、动画中等": viewers could name the field and could not see the knowledge, because film knowledge lives in who stands where, who looks at whom and what the light does. Every kit therefore carries two more slots: **Register** (main register and range, `style-dna.md` B6: R1 signs · R2 cutaway · R3 silhouette stage · R4 cut paper) and **Inhabitants** (the concrete things the knowledge lives on, which must be drawn). Test: swap the inhabitants for dots — if the lesson survives, R1 is enough; if it dies, draw them. The table below gives every ledger field a first-pass register; kits written before v4 get theirs from it until their seed pack is revised.

## High-School Curriculum Kits (高考刚需 — the strongest mass hook)

Why these are separate from the general-subject kits: the same subject is two different products. General math sells "the beauty of math" to curious viewers; high-school math visualizes the exam syllabus for students and parents. The gaokao is the hardest mass hook on the Chinese internet, and each syllabus IS the topic pool (100+ knowledge points per subject — a three-year series that never runs dry).

## Engineering-Course Kits (传热学/工程力学 etc.)

Engineering courses are a structurally privileged fit: their core difficulty — invisible quantities (stress, heat flux, current, flow) — is exactly what this style visualizes; their graphic language is already standardized (FBD, mechanism diagrams, circuit symbols, contour maps); and their case ladders end in product teardowns (phone vapor chambers, folding hinges, train noses), the same commercial pattern as Shenhai's Huawei/BMW episodes.

Shared engineering cautions: max ONE short formula per beat, carry derivations with shapes; replace rainbow simulation colormaps with the red/blue semantic pair (hot/cold, tension/compression — already legal in the style rules); describe standard symbols by geometry ("a zigzag resistor symbol"), not just by name; prefer relative comparisons (doubles, halves) over precise numeric tables.

## Anti-Fit Domains

Food review (needs real appetite texture), travel scenery (needs real footage), emotional stories/interviews (needs faces), breaking news (style hurts credibility), physical skill micro-teaching like gym form or fingering (needs live demonstration; principle episodes OK, tutorial episodes not).

## Five-Step Transplant Checklist (any new field)

1. Find the alphabet: what are this field's "dots/lines/planes"?
2. Fix the anchor kit, the inhabitants and the register: 3-5 instantly recognizable containers/notations, present in every beat; the concrete things the knowledge lives on (people, objects, works), which must be drawn; the main register and range (`style-dna.md` B6, the dots test).
3. Pick morph chains: prefer evolution/growth/contrast knowledge points (they exploit zero-cut morphing best).
4. Build the case ladder: historic masterpiece → classic application → modern product/app.
5. Apply the seven-part skeleton: paired cold open → attribute contrast → cases → concept cards → interaction plant → aphorism → tri-icon outro.
