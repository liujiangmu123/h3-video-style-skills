# Hit Mechanics — why Shenhai's films get watched, saved and shared

Style rules (how a Shenhai film looks and moves) live in `style-dna.md`. This file answers a different question: **which of those choices produce the audience response**, so that an episode can keep the mechanism even when the surface varies, and so that "innovation" can be judged by whether it strengthens or weakens a mechanism instead of by taste.

Evidence base: the 13 originals in `设计师深海视频分析\风格分析工作区\` (frame-level re-read 2026-09-05, per-film cards in `设计师深海_白底极简科普\设计师深海视频分析\逐帧核心提炼_2026-09-05.md` §1), the production line's 84 A/B/C-graded episode reviews distilled in `深海风格量产\07_旁白升级规范.md`, and 2026 platform-weight research (`shenhai-series-planner/references/rating-rubric.md` §4 holds the numbers; not repeated here). Local files carry **no play counts** — every claim below is a mechanism inferred from structure plus platform weights, not a measured lift.

## 1. What the titles do

The 13 real titles, in original order:

糟了我成替身了 · 人人都该了解的10个设计常识 · 两个蝙蝠侠接吻背后是流传千年的设计美学 · 这条线全是细节！ · 正-VS-斜! · 未来竟然是这样！？ · 我又相信光了！ · 什么叫升维打击？ · 设计中的「条条框框」 · 这才是手机屏幕的正确打开方式! · 二笔画 · 反正你一定会看 · 我有个绝妙的idea，就差...

Pattern: a meme or idiom borrowed from outside design (替身 / 相信光 / 升维打击), a pun that only resolves after watching (条条框框 = literal lines and planes; 反正 = flip-flop ambigram), a contrast pair (正 VS 斜), a negation or dare (反正你一定会看), a list promise (10 个常识). **No title contains a design term.** The title sells the reversal, the film delivers the term at 12–27 s after the demonstration.

## 2. The five mechanisms

| # | Mechanism | What the film does | Audience response it buys | Where the rule sits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cognitive gap at 0.3 s | A declarative sentence about a concrete thing on screen ("这是一条线"), no greeting, no music intro; 12/13 films start speaking within 0.14–0.30 s | 3-second retention: the viewer is inside the argument before deciding whether to leave | A8, A9, B-0 rule 7 |
| 2 | Show before name | The phenomenon runs first; the bilingual term card lands only after the viewer has watched it happen | The "原来如此" moment is experienced as the viewer's own discovery, not received as a fact — this is the comment/share trigger ("我居然自己看出来了") | A9.1, B-0 rule 2 |
| 3 | Evidence with skeleton | Real logos, paintings, photos with construction lines drawn over them (V05: seven logos with dashed skeletons) | Trust: the principle is proven on things the viewer already recognizes, so the video feels like knowledge rather than opinion | A9.2, B-0 rule 4 |
| 4 | Back in the viewer's hand | The last example is the viewer's own like button / screen / handwriting (V05 tilts the like icon, V12 makes 点赞 an ambigram) | Ownership → save. "This applies to the thing in my hand" is the reason to collect for later — the 2026 first-weight signal | A9.3, B-0 rule 6 |
| 5 | Stillness under a continuous voice | Picture frozen 70–88 % of the time, one moving element, one accent; voice never pauses over 1 s | Zero fatigue: the ear is held, the eye is rested, so 150–270 s completes without strain (completion rate) | A6, B-0 rule 5 |

Two secondary mechanisms that the production line confirmed:

- **Restraint as memorability.** The closing aphorism is one calm sentence, never a slogan or three-point recap (`07_旁白升级规范.md` §一-5). The absence of a "creator ending" is itself what viewers quote back.
- **Sponsorship without shrinking the lesson.** 6/13 originals are sponsored and all use the same structure: full-length principle first, product second; the principle half is numerically indistinguishable from the pure-principle films. The audience tolerates the ad because the lesson was not cut short — never shorten the mechanism half to make room for a product (A9.7).

## 3. Hook pattern library (opening lines that scored A in production review)

Use to write the first two sentences and to rewrite a textbook-sounding title. Examples are verbatim production lines (channel · episode).

| # | Pattern | Verbatim example | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 实物直入 Object first | "这是一颗珠子。/一颗珠子，不是纹样。/一圈珠子，就是了。"（传统纹样 024） | Principle episodes; Shenhai's signature — but ≤2 of any 5 consecutive episodes |
| 2 | 认知翻转 Flip a belief | "你以为它是平的。放大看——它在爬坡。"（汉字 004）; short form "写字二十年，你可能一直被骗。" | Correcting common knowledge |
| 3 | 悬念反转 Suspense then reversal | "影史最著名的一场屠杀。/发生在一段阶梯上。/但它，从未发生过。"（电影 021） | History / debunking; "影史最 X" ≤1 per 10 episodes |
| 4 | 数字反差 Number contrast | "二十美元，拍出了两百块。没人作弊，全程自愿。"（博弈论 007 short） | Experiments, data |
| 5 | 直指痛点 / 挑战 Dare | "手里这颗鸡蛋，敢使劲捏吗？"（工程力学 006）; "这个数，比一大吗？高考给你三秒。"（高中数学 008） | Skills, exam points |
| 6 | 新鲜类比 Fresh analogy | "这是一块龟甲。三千年前的一条朋友圈。"（汉字 006） | Then-vs-now; analogy must stay an analogy, never a factual claim |
| 7 | 反高潮自答 Anticlimax answer | "但它是谁发明的？——答案是：不知道。"（汉字 008） | Origins, attribution |
| 8 | 感官验证 Sensory check | "把机械表贴近耳朵。滴答，滴答。每一秒，整整八声。"（机械原理 023） | Anything the viewer can verify on the spot; audio-native fields |

Graded-down openings (real cases): background announcements — "今天讲：化学平衡", "一九八五年，第一关开幕"; pure setup sentences that postpone the reversal — "三扇门，选对开走一辆车。" (the real hook, "上千名博士答错", arrived at 8 s). Short-form first sentence must be a question / contrast / negation; long-form must open the cognitive gap within two sentences. The title's promise must be caught by those two sentences — no swapping topics, no late pickup.

**Voice comes before mechanism (style-dna B-0.7, A8.9).** The 13 originals never open with a question, a greeting or a dare — the first sentence is a statement, in a teacher's voice. So in the **long version** every pattern above is delivered as statements: pattern 5 becomes "手里这颗鸡蛋，使劲捏。捏不碎。" not "敢使劲捏吗？"; pattern 7 keeps its self-answer but drops the rhetorical build-up ("发明它的人，没人知道。"). The cognitive gap is preserved — it lives in the contradiction between two statements, not in the question mark. Question-form openers are legal **only in the 抖音 short version**, where the platform's first-second rule outranks the channel voice; even there, one question, then statements.

## 4. Which of these an episode may vary

Innovation is allowed only where it *strengthens* a mechanism above; tier 1 of the B-0 table is the physical form of mechanisms 1, 2 and 5 and is therefore not negotiable. The register (`style-dna.md` B6) is not an innovation tier: it is derived for every episode, A, B or C, because mechanism 3 (evidence) and mechanism 4 (the viewer's hand) only fire when the viewer recognizes the real thing.

- **A 常规**: all five mechanisms via the default recipe; originality lives in the domain material and the case ladder.
- **B 母题创新**: one graphic device carried through the whole film (V11's stroke-by-stroke lines, V05's dashed skeletons) — strengthens mechanism 3 and 5 without touching the numbers.
- **C 形式即内容**: the form performs the topic (V12's screen flips with the ambigram; V04 draws its own title out of the line it teaches). This is the single strongest "wow" source (B-0 rule 1) and the only tier where `creative-animation-treatment` is invoked in whole-film mode — hand it the B-0 right column as unbreakable constraints. Reserve for episodes with the strongest hook; more than a few per season dilutes the surprise.

The tier for each episode arrives from the planning layer in the 任务消息 (创新档 field). When there is no upstream brief, pick A unless the topic's own material makes B or C obvious in the 创意五问 answer to question 4.

## 5. Self-test before delivering a script

1. Read the first two sentences aloud: is there a thing on screen and a gap in the viewer's head? If it is an announcement, rewrite with §3.
2. Write the viewer's "原来如此" sentence in one line. If it is a restatement of common knowledge, the episode has no mechanism 2 — change the principle.
3. Name the real object the skeleton is drawn on (mechanism 3) and the object in the viewer's hand (mechanism 4). Missing 4 is allowed but must be flagged "无 A9.3 收尾". The evidence must be recognizable in the episode's register (`style-dna.md` B6): a famous film scene is re-staged with the silhouette cast and its one signature prop, not replaced by a name card.
4. Count events against sentences (mechanism 5): at most one event per sentence and at least a third of sentences still — but "fewer than sentences" is a ceiling, not the target; the film still reaches the style's `target_events_per_min` (17/min since 10-01, pixel metric, about 0.6–0.7 events per sentence; `h3-storyboard-writing` §6, `h3-production-lessons` F-001).
5. Closing line: no imperative, no wish, no recap.
6. Completeness: the gap opened by the first two sentences is closed at the end; at least one sentence says *why*; the opening image returns with its meaning changed.
7. Frame test: pause on any three frames — would one of them work as the cover? If every frame is an empty canvas with a few icons in a field whose knowledge lives in people, places or light, mechanism 3 and the save trigger both fail; re-derive the register (`style-dna.md` B6).
